Carmelina Soto

Carmelina Soto Valencia (1916–1994) was a Colombian poet, born in Armenia, Quindío. Some of her well-known works include Bells of Dawn, October and Motionless Time.

Biography

Carmelina Soto Carmelina Soto Valencia (1916–1994) was a Colombian poet, born in Armenia, Quindío. Some of her well-known works include Bells of Dawn, October and Motionless Time.
