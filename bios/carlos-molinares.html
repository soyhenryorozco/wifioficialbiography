Carlos Molinares

Carlos Molinares Saumeth (born 23 June 1956) is a Colombian former footballer who played as a forward. He competed in the men's tournament at the 1980 Summer Olympics. At club level, he represented Atlético Junior. Molinares was known for his speed and his dribbling.

Biography

Carlos Molinares Carlos Molinares Saumeth (born 23 June 1956) is a Colombian former footballer who played as a forward. He competed in the men's tournament at the 1980 Summer Olympics. At club level, he represented Atlético Junior. Molinares was known for his speed and his dribbling.
