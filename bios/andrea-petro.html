Biography

Andrea Petro Andrea Petro Herrán (born March 29, 1991) is a Colombian economist and businesswoman. A member of the Petro family, she is the eldest daughter of Gustavo Petro, the 37th president of Colombia, and his first wife, Mary Luz Herrán. Born in Bogotá, D.C., she attended the Lycée Français Louis Pasteur while her father served as a Member of the Chamber of Representatives for Bogotá. Petro holds a degree in economics from the University of Aix-Marseille III. She is the CEO of the Bachué Sportswear brand.
