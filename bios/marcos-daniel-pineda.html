Biography

Marcos Daniel Pineda Marcos Daniel Pineda García (born June 5, 1977) is a Colombian business administrator, politician and Senator of Colombia who previously served as Mayor of Montería twice from 2008 to 2011 and again from 2016 to 2019. A member of the Conservative Party, Pineda received the third-highest vote in 2022. Born in Montería, Córdoba, Pineda graduated from Sergio Arboleda University in Bogotá, D.C. He began his political career as Mayor of Montería, elected in 2007 and re-elected in 2015.
