Biography

Andrés Julián Rendón Andrés Julián Rendón Cardona (born 9 January 1978) is a Colombian politician and public administrator. From 2001 to 2006 he was a councilor of Rionegro, and from 2008 to 2011 was Secretary of the Government of Rionegro. He later served as Mayor of Rionegro from 2016 to 2019, and was most recently elected as Governor of Antioquia, in the 2023 regional elections.
