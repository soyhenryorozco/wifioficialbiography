Biography

Hernando Osorio Jaime Hernando Osorio (born 27 September 1953 in Ibagué) is a Colombian painter and print-maker. Educated at the National University of Colombia, he was a member of the Awuyaka street art group. During his New York City period (1981–1985) he focused on etching and lithography. Since 1985 he has been living and working in Vienna, Austria. He is a member of Galerie Sur. His work is represented in the following collections: Victoria and Albert Museum, London Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris The Library of Congress Print Collection, Washington D.C. Graphische Sammlung Albertina, Vienna, Austria The New York Public Library, New York, N.Y.
