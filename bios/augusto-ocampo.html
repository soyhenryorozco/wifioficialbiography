Biography

Augusto Ocampo Augusto Alfonso Ocampo Camacho (born c. 1974) is a Colombian lawyer and attorney who has served as Legal Secretary to the Presidency since 2025. Ocampo graduated with a law degree from the Catholic University of Colombia. He subsequently joined Gustavo Petro's 2011 mayoral campaign in Bogotá. Later, he was appointed District Director of Disciplinary Affairs during Petro's term as mayor of Bogotá. In 2016, he was appointed Director of the Transparency Office of Ibagué under Mayor Guillermo Alfonso Jaramillo. In 2022, he joined Gustavo Petro's 2022 presidential campaign. In April 2025, Petro appointed him Legal Secretary of the Presidency. From June to October 2025, he served as Acting Minister of Justice following the resignation of Ángela María Buitrago.
