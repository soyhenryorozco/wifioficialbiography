Biography

Yeimar Mosquera Yeimar Jesús Mosquera Mosquera (born 6 February 2005) is a Colombian footballer who plays as a centre-back for Primeira Liga club Vitória. He is a product of the Deportivo Pereira academy and played in the Colombian second tier for Orsomarso. He signed for Premier League side Aston Villa in 2024, where he appeared for their youth sides and spent time on loan at sister club Real Unión. Mosquera transferred to Vitória in July 2026. Mosquera has been a regular for the Colombia U20 side and was a key player in their third place finish at the 2025 FIFA U-20 World Cup in Chile and the 2025 South American U-20 Championship in Venezuela, also finishing third.
