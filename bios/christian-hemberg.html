Christian Hemberg

Christian Andres Hemberg (born 6 December 1981) is a Swedish former professional footballer who played as a forward. He played for Örgryte, Raufoss, and KS/Leiftur during a career that spanned between 1998 and 2008. He won two caps for the Sweden national team in 2001.

Biography

Christian Hemberg Christian Andres Hemberg (born 6 December 1981) is a Swedish former professional footballer who played as a forward. He played for Örgryte, Raufoss, and KS/Leiftur during a career that spanned between 1998 and 2008. He won two caps for the Sweden national team in 2001.
