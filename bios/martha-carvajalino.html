Biography

Martha Carvajalino Martha Viviana Carvajalino Villegas (born 6 February 1983) is a Colombian lawyer and politician who served as Deputy Minister of Rural Development from 2023 to 2024 and as Minister of Agriculture and Rural Development since 8 July 2024. Born in Bogotá, D.C., she studied law at the National University of Colombia, where she also earned a master's degree in Constitutional Law.
