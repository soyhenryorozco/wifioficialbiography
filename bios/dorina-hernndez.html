Biography

Dorina Hernández Dorina Hernández Palomino (born 23 July 1966) is a Colombian educator, cultural manager, politician, and a member of the Chamber of Representatives. A member of Soy Porque Somos, Hernández is one of the highest-ranking members within the party. Hernández has served as an advisor to the Ministry of National Education and as President of Soy Porque Somos. Born in Cartagena, Bolívar, Hernández led the quest for San Basilio de Palenque to be recognized as Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity by UNESCO in 2005. In 2022, she became the first Palenquera woman to be elected to Congress.
