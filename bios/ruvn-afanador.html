Biography

Ruvén Afanador Ruvén Afanador (sometimes spelled Ruven Afanador; born 1959, Bucaramanga, Colombia) is a Colombian-American photographer known for fashion photography and celebrity portraits. His work has appeared in Vogue, Vanity Fair, and The New York Times Magazine, and he has photographed subjects including Jennifer Aniston, Bill Clinton, and Rihanna. Afanador has also published monographs on Spanish flamenco and Colombian indigenous communities.
