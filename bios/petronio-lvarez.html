Biography

Petronio Álvarez Patricio Romano Petronio Álvarez Quintero (1914–1966), known as Petronio Álvarez, was a Colombian musician and songwriter. He is known as the King of Currulao, and his song "Mi Buenaventura" has been called an anthem of the city of Buenaventura. The Petronio Álvarez Festival, held annually in Cali since 1997, was named in his honour.
