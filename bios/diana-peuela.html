Biography

Diana Peñuela Diana Carolina Peñuela Martínez (born 8 September 1986) is a Colombian racing cyclist, who currently rides for UCI Women's Continental Team DNA Pro Cycling. She is known as a sprinter with victories in Criterium Manizales Fair, the Rigorena Circuit, Grand Prix Armenia, Diablo Twilight Criterium, Milwaukee Mile Crit and Bronze medals in the XVII Bolivarian Games in 2013 and the X South American Games in 2014. She competed in the 2013 UCI women's road race in Florence, and the 2015 UCI Road World Championships in Richmond.
