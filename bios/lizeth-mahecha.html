Lizeth Mahecha

Lizeth Yamile Mahecha Arévalo (December 6, 1971) is a Colombian lawyer and beauty pageant titleholder who was crowned Miss Colombia 1989 and competed at Miss Universe 1990 where she placed 2nd runner-up.

Biography

Lizeth Mahecha Lizeth Yamile Mahecha Arévalo (December 6, 1971) is a Colombian lawyer and beauty pageant titleholder who was crowned Miss Colombia 1989 and competed at Miss Universe 1990 where she placed 2nd runner-up.
