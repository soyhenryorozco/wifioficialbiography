Biography

Jeison Suárez Jeison Alexander Suárez Bocanegra (born 21 March 1991) is a male Long-distance runner from Colombia. He won the gold medal in the men's half marathon at the 2017 Bolivarian Games and the men's marathon at the 2018 Central American and Caribbean Games. He represented Colombia at the 2020 Summer Olympics.
