Diego Pinzón

Diego Juan Pinzón Florez (born 12 February 1985) is a Colombian racewalking athlete. He represented Colombia at the 2020 Summer Olympics in Tokyo 2021, placing 18th in the men's 50 kilometres walk.

Biography

Diego Pinzón Diego Juan Pinzón Florez (born 12 February 1985) is a Colombian racewalking athlete. He represented Colombia at the 2020 Summer Olympics in Tokyo 2021, placing 18th in the men's 50 kilometres walk.
