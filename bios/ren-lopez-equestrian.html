Biography

René Lopez (equestrian) René López Lizarazo (born 5 April 1964) is a Colombian Olympic show jumping rider. He competed at the 2016 Summer Olympics in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, where he finished 55th in the individual competition. López also competed at the 2006 World Equestrian Games, the 2015 Pan American Games, the 2023 Pan American Games, and the 2024 Summer Olympics.
