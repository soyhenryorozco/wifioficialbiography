Biography

Henry Zambrano Henry Zambrano Sandoval (born August 7, 1973) is a retired soccer player who played for the Colombia national team. He first made his name at the 1993 World Youth Cup where he was the Golden Shoe winner—the award for the best player in the tournament. He later moved onward to senior national team, where he played between 1994 and 1999. For his club career, he played for Atlético Nacional, MetroStars, D.C. United, Colorado Rapids and Deportes Quindío.
