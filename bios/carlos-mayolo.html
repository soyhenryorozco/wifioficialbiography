Carlos Mayolo

Carlos Mayolo (10 September 1945 – 3 February 2007) was a Colombian actor and film director. He directed more than ten films from 1970 to 2000. The style of his films was defined by himself as "Tropical Gothic".

Biography

Carlos Mayolo Carlos Mayolo (10 September 1945 – 3 February 2007) was a Colombian actor and film director. He directed more than ten films from 1970 to 2000. The style of his films was defined by himself as "Tropical Gothic".
