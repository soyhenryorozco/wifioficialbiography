Biography

Andrés Botero Phillipsbourne Andrés Botero Phillipsbourne (born December 23, 1945) is a Colombian mechanical engineer, businessman and sports leader. He inherited the passion for sports activities from his father, Oscar Botero, born in Paris, France, who married the British Muriel Catherine Philipsbourne and years later moved to Colombia, where Andrés was born. From the early age of 6 years, Botero began practicing sports activities, more specifically, shotgun shooting and horseback riding. Throughout his life, Botero has always had a passion for sports and other physically demanding activities, having practiced hunting, horse riding, aviation, rallying, motorboating and motorcycling, among others.
