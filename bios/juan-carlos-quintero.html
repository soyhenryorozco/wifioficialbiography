Biography

Juan Carlos Quintero Juan Carlos Quintero Pérez (born February 20, 1978) is a Colombian retired footballer who played as a midfielder. He began his career coming up from Envigado's youth ranks and made his professional debut in the 1999 season. He played at many top Colombian clubs like Millonarios, Atlético Junior, Independiente Medellín, and Independiente Santa Fe. Some of the best spells of his career were at Junior, where he was part of the championship squad in the 2004 Clausura, and with Independiente Santa Fe where he won the 2009 Copa Colombia.
