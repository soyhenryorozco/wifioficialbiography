Biography

Gil Colunje Gil Colunje (1 September 1831 – 6 January 1899) was a Colombian politician, lawyer, journalist, romantic writer, and poet. He was an MP in the Federal State of Panama in 1865, magistrate of the Supreme Court of Justice of Colombia. Colunje took part in the Colombian Civil War of 1854 in Bogotá.
