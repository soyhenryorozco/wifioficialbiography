Lady Ruano

Lady Ruano (born March 5, 1981) is a Colombian table tennis player. She competed at the 2016 Summer Olympics in the women's singles event, in which she was eliminated in the first round by Iveta Vacenovská.

Biography

Lady Ruano Lady Ruano (born March 5, 1981) is a Colombian table tennis player. She competed at the 2016 Summer Olympics in the women's singles event, in which she was eliminated in the first round by Iveta Vacenovská.
