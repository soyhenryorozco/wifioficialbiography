Biography

Nidia Góngora Nidia Sofía Góngora Bonilla (born 1979 or 1980) is a Colombian singer and songwriter. Góngora writes and sings in traditional styles of music from the Colombian Pacific. She has released several albums with her group Canalón de Timbiquí and as a solo artist, most recently Pacífico Maravilla in 2025.
