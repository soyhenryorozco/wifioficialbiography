Almes Granados

Almes Guillermo Granados Melo (born 1958 or 1959) is a Colombian vallenato accordionist. He was crowned vallenato king for winning the accordionist competition of the Vallenato Legend Festival in 2011, and in 2022 he won the king of kings competition.

Biography

Almes Granados Almes Guillermo Granados Melo (born 1958 or 1959) is a Colombian vallenato accordionist. He was crowned vallenato king for winning the accordionist competition of the Vallenato Legend Festival in 2011, and in 2022 he won the king of kings competition.
