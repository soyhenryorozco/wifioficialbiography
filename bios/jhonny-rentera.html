Jhonny Rentería

Jhonny Alexander Rentería Jiménez (born 26 March 1997) is a Colombian sprinter. He has won several medals at regional level. His personal bests are 9.97 seconds in the 100 metres (+1.5 m/s, Bilbao 2024) and 20.73 seconds in the 200 metres (+1.9 m/s, Quito 2025).
