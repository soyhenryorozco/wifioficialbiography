Biography

Melissa Gonzalez (hurdler) Melissa Blough (née Gonzalez; born June 24, 1994) is a Colombian American athlete specializing in the 400 meters hurdles and 4 × 400 meters relay. A native of El Paso, Texas, U.S., she also holds Colombian citizenship and represents them at international events. She has won several gold medals, mostly in South American competitions and set a personal best of 55.32 seconds at the 2020 Summer Olympics. Gonzalez is married to National Football League (NFL) coach and former player David Blough while her younger brother Christian Gonzalez plays cornerback in the same league.
