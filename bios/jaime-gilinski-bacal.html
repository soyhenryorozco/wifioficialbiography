Jaime Gilinski Bacal

Jaime Gilinski Bacal (born 14 December 1957) is a Panama-based Colombian banker, investor, and real estate developer. According to Forbes, he is the richest person in Colombia, with a net worth of US$15.1 billion as of 2026.
