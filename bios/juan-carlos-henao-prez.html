Biography

Juan Carlos Henao Pérez Juan Carlos Henao Pérez (6 December 1959 – 2 January 2024) was a Colombian lawyer and jurist. He served as president of the Constitutional Court from 2009 to 2010. He was part of the team that reached the Colombian peace agreement. Henao died in Bogotá on 2 January 2024, at the age of 64.
