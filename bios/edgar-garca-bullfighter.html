Biography

Edgar García (bullfighter) Edgar García (1 January 1960 – 21 November 2020) more commonly known as El Dandy, was a Colombian bullfighter. Born in Zarzal, Colombia, he took the alternative on 22 January 1995 in the bullring of Manizales, Colombia being godfather José Antonio Campuzano and witness Tomás Campuzano. He confirmed the alternative on 29 December 2002 in Plaza de Toros México from the hands of Mexican bullfighter Mariano Ramos as godfather and Jorge Mora as his witness. He also dedicated himself to calling festivals so that marginalized young people could participate alongside him so that they could show their bullfighting arts.
