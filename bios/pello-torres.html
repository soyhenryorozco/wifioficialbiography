Pello Torres

Pedro Manuel Torres Arroyo (1924–2008), known as Pello Torres or Peyo Torres, was a Colombian trumpeter and songwriter. Torres wrote "El Culebro", which played an important role in the development of porro as a commercially successful genre in Colombia, and he founded and led the band Los Diablos del Ritmo.

Biography

Pello Torres Pedro Manuel Torres Arroyo (1924–2008), known as Pello Torres or Peyo Torres, was a Colombian trumpeter and songwriter. Torres wrote "El Culebro", which played an important role in the development of porro as a commercially successful genre in Colombia, and he founded and led the band Los Diablos del Ritmo.
