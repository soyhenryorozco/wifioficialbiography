Biography

Eduardo Santos Montejo Eduardo Santos Montejo (August 28, 1888 – March 27, 1974) was a Colombian publisher and politician who was President of Colombia from 1938 to 1942. He was active in the Colombian Liberal Party. He purchased the prominent Bogotá newspaper El Tiempo in 1913; El Tiempo was founded by his brother-in-law Alfonso Villegas Restrepo in 1911. Members of the Santos family were the main shareholders of the paper until 2007. He served as the President of Colombia from August 1938 to August 1942, having been elected without opposition. During his presidency, a number of reforms were carried out in areas such as working conditions and housing.
