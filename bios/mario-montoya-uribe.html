Biography

Mario Montoya Uribe Mario Montoya Uribe (born 29 April 1949) is a former military general and commander of the Colombian National Army (Spanish: Ejército Nacional de Colombia), until his resignation on November 4, 2008, following the "false positives" scandal, involving the deaths of 11 civilians at the hands of the military. Montoya holds a graduate title in senior management from Los Andes University. He has trained in armored vehicle courses in Fort Knox, Kentucky and served as the army, navy and air attaché at the Colombian embassy in London, England. Montoya was succeeded by General Óscar González on November 6, 2008, as commander of the Colombian National Army.
