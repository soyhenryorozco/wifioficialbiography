Biography

Elsa Noguera Elsa Margarita Noguera de la Espriella (born 30 September 1973) is a Colombian politician. She was the vice presidential nominee for the Radical Change Party in 2010, and in 2012 she was elected mayor of Barranquilla. She later served as the Minister of Housing, City and Territory. In 2020 she was elected the governor of the Atlántico Department. Noguera received a degree in International Business from the American University. She also obtained an MBA from the University of the North (Uninorte).
