Natalia Valenzuela

Natalia Valenzuela Cutiva (born April 16, 1989 in Neiva) is a Colombian TV host, model and beauty pageant titleholder who won the Miss Huila 2010 contest which allowed her to participate in the Miss Colombia 2010 pageant, where she won the title of Primera Princesa (2nd runner-up) and Miss Colombia International.

Biography

Natalia Valenzuela Natalia Valenzuela Cutiva (born April 16, 1989 in Neiva) is a Colombian TV host, model and beauty pageant titleholder who won the Miss Huila 2010 contest which allowed her to participate in the Miss Colombia 2010 pageant, where she won the title of Primera Princesa (2nd runner-up) and Miss Colombia International.
