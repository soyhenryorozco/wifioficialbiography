Biography

Orber Aquiles Moreno Sulbarán (born April 27, 1977) is a Venezuelan former professional baseball relief pitcher. He played in Major League Baseball (MLB) for the Kansas City Royals and New York Mets in parts of three seasons spanning 1999–2004. Listed at 6 ft 3 in (1.91 m), 225 pounds (102 kg), Moreno batted and threw right-handed.
