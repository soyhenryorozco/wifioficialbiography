Carlos Cuco Rojas

Carlos Rojas Hernández (San Martín, Meta), better known as Carlos "Cuco" Rojas, (28 March 1954 – 10 January 2020) was a Colombian harpist and songwriter of joropo. He was the director of Colombian band Cimarron.
