Armando Silva

Armando Silva (born August 19, 1948 in Bogotá) is a Colombian philosopher, semiotician and writer, who is best known for his work on Urban Imaginaries, which was developed in several cities in Latin America, Spain, the United States and other European cities.

Biography

Armando Silva Armando Silva (born August 19, 1948 in Bogotá) is a Colombian philosopher, semiotician and writer, who is best known for his work on Urban Imaginaries, which was developed in several cities in Latin America, Spain, the United States and other European cities.
