Biography

Juan Daniel Oviedo Juan Daniel Oviedo Arango (born 16 March 1977) is a Colombian economist and politician. From 2018 to 2022, he served as director of the National Administrative Department of Statistics. From 2024 to 2025, he was a city councillor of Bogotá. In the 2023 municipal elections, he was a candidate for mayor of Bogotá. In the 2026 presidential election, he was a candidate for vice president of Colombia.
