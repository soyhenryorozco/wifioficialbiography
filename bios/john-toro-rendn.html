Biography

John Toro Rendón John Jairo Toro Rendón (born April 4, 1958) is a retired Colombian football referee. He is known for having refereed one match in the 1998 FIFA World Cup in France, the Group C match between Denmark and South Africa, during which he handed out seven yellow cards and three reds. In addition, he was a referee at the 1991 FIFA Women's World Cup.
