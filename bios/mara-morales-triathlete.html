María Morales (triathlete)

María Carmenza Morales Rendón (born July 21, 1966) is an athlete from Colombia, who competes in triathlon. Morales competed at the first Olympic triathlon at the 2000 Summer Olympics. She took thirty-seventh place with a total time of 2:13:43.38.

Biography

María Morales (triathlete) María Carmenza Morales Rendón (born July 21, 1966) is an athlete from Colombia, who competes in triathlon. Morales competed at the first Olympic triathlon at the 2000 Summer Olympics. She took thirty-seventh place with a total time of 2:13:43.38.
