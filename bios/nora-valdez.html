Biography

Nora Valdez Nora Valdez (born 9 September 1997) is a Colombian compound archer. She won the gold medal in the women's team event at the 2017 World Archery Championships held in Mexico City, Mexico. In 2021, she also won the gold medal in the women's team event at the World Archery Championships held in Yankton, United States.
