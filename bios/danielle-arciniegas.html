Biography

Danielle Arciniegas Danielle Arciniegas Martínez is a Colombian actress, model, and singer. She is known for her participation in the television productions: Esmeraldas and Hilos de sangre azul and in the Nickelodeon Latin America series Yo soy Franky, where she plays the role of Tamara Franco and in Francisco el Matemático: Clase 2017 with the role of María Mónica Sánchez 'Barby's. In 2016 Arciniegas won the Chica Trendy award at the third installment of the Kids Choice Awards Colombia.
