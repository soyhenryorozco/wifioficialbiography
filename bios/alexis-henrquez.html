Biography

Alexis Henríquez Alexis Hector Henriquez Charales (1 February 1983) is a Colombian retired professional footballer who played as a defender. Henriquez is a two time winner of the Copa Libertadores. He first won the title with Once Caldas in 2004 and captained Atlético Nacional to its second title in 2016.
