Biography

Shakira videography Colombian singer-songwriter Shakira has released 95 music videos, 5 live albums, 8 films, and 4 documentaries. In 1991, Shakira signed a recording contract with Sony Music Colombia and released her debut album Magia (1991). Three music videos were directed for the album. Only one music video was directed for her second album, Peligro. Shakira has refused to allow the re-release of any videos from her first two albums due to their "immaturity". In 1994, Shakira made her acting debut playing Luisa Maria in the Colombian telenovela El Oasis. Shakira's first major music video was for "Estoy Aquí", directed by Simón Brand, for her third album Pies Descalzos.
