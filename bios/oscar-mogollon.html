Oscar Mogollon

Oscar Mogollón Jaimes (1940 – 18 October 2009) was a Colombian educator. He was an education visionary of the late 20th century who pioneered the Escuela Nueva model and the Active school approach, educational models that have been used to improve the quality of education of the poorest children around the world.

Biography

Oscar Mogollon Oscar Mogollón Jaimes (1940 – 18 October 2009) was a Colombian educator. He was an education visionary of the late 20th century who pioneered the Escuela Nueva model and the Active school approach, educational models that have been used to improve the quality of education of the poorest children around the world.
