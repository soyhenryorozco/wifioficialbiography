Biography

Freddy León Freddy Alberto León Aristizábal (born 24 September 1970) is a retired Colombian football striker. He was born in Bogotá. He spent the main part of his career in Millonarios from 1990 through 2000, except a spell in Deportes Tolima from 1996 through 1998. Between 2002 and 2007 he played short spells for lesser clubs; Centauros Villavicencio, Patriotas FC, Cortuluá and Expreso Rojo. He was capped 8 times for Colombia national football team in 1995, including at the 1995 Copa América.
