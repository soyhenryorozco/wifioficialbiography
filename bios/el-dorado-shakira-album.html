Biography

El Dorado (Shakira album) El Dorado (English: The Golden, Spanish: [el̪ d̪oˈɾa.ð̞o]) is the eleventh studio album by Colombian singer-songwriter Shakira, released on 26 May 2017, by Sony Music Latin. The album is mainly sung in Spanish, with three songs in English. After her self-titled tenth studio album (2014), Shakira had her second child, suffered from writer's block and was uncertain about the future of her career. However, her collaboration on "La Bicicleta" with Carlos Vives, encouraged Shakira to continue to work on music. Inspired by her personal life and experiences, El Dorado primarily features songs about the singer's relationship with Piqué.
