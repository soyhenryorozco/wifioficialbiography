Biography

María del Socorro Bustamante María del Socorro Bustamante (June 24, 1955 – March 21, 2015) was a Colombian politician and physiotherapist who served as a councilor on the Cartagena City Council from 2000 to 2011. Initially, she started her career in healthcare by specializing in respiratory physiotherapy and intensive care, eventually serving as the head of the therapy department at the Nuevo Hospital Bocagrande. She later transitioned into politics by becoming a member of the Junior Chamber of Cartagena and serving as the mayor of Locality 1 in 1994. During her subsequent career, she served on the Cartagena City Council representing the People's Will Movement and held the position of council president in 2002.
