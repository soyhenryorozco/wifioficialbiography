Biography

Rocio Restrepo Rocío del Pilar Restrepo Lugo (born November 21, 1987) is a Colombian professional ten-pin bowler who now resides in the United States. She bowled collegiately for Wichita State University and now bowls professionally on the Professional Women's Bowling Association (PWBA) Tour. She also bowls internationally as a member of Team Colombia. After 11 years as a member of the Roto Grip pro staff, Restrepo signed with Hammer Bowling in January, 2026. She is also sponsored by VISE Grip inserts and Logo Infusion.
