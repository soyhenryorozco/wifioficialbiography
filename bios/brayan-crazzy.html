Gaming Free Fire Streaming Content Creation Video Games Colombia Influencer

Brayan Crazzy (born October 15, 2000) is a Colombian gamer, streamer and digital content creator, considered one of the largest Free Fire content creators in Colombia. Born in Cartagena, Bolívar, Colombia, under the name Brayan Jesús Orozco Durango, he built a personal brand that consolidated playing Operation7 and later Free Fire, the battle royale game by Garena that made him an international reference in gaming content.

With a community of more than 4 million followers across his social networks (TikTok, Facebook, YouTube and Instagram), Brayan Crazzy has collaborated with brands such as Garena Free Fire, Nubia Technology, Delta Force and Temu, participated in the Free Fire Awards in Mexico, and has been invited to international gaming conventions, including events in Uzbekistan.

Early life and beginnings

Brayan Crazzy was born on October 15, 2000, in Cartagena, department of Bolívar, Colombia, in the La Primera de Mayo neighborhood, near Santa Rita. He grew up in a working-class environment and, from a very young age, turned to the internet as a concrete path of progress.

At the age of 14, in his native Cartagena, he began a successful career as a digital brand positioner for businesses and as a creator of humorous content on the internet under the name "Brayan de Jesús". His accounts grew organically week after week — empirically and organically, without formal training — positioning brands and communities for foreign clients who trusted his creative skills.

However, when his largest account was removed without explanation, he reoriented his career toward another of his passions: gamer streams — live broadcasts of combat video game communities, such as the popular Free Fire, which allow multiple competitors from anywhere in the world to participate simultaneously.

Career

Digital evolution and personal brand

Brayan Crazzy began in the digital market at age 14 by managing Facebook pages and growing communities for clients abroad, generating significant income at that stage of his life. It all started on Facebook, when he installed his first game, Dragon City, and later moved on to Operation7, where he began consolidating his personal brand as Brayan Crazzy.

Photo-based and video content gave him visibility as a humorous creator, but his definitive leap came when he risked showing himself as he really was: extroverted, jokey and irreverent — exactly what he shows daily in his Free Fire streams. Today the Brayan Crazzy brand also extends to digital entrepreneurship, monetizing platforms such as Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.

Gaming streams and Free Fire

Free Fire is the game that catapulted him as one of the largest gaming content creators in Colombia, with a community of more than 4 million followers under the handle @BrayanCrazzy. His content combines gameplay highlights, humor and direct interaction with his community through live streams ("en vivos").

He has recorded content with major Free Fire creators, including @DejavuFF, @SantanaNotFace, @SmookeFF and @Tinca, as well as with @CreckGamer, a YouTuber with more than 10 million subscribers, and @Corviff, a TikToker with a similar audience. He dreams of one day recording with @Donato, considered the biggest Free Fire creator.

For a typical week, he dedicates himself to recording three- or four-hour streaming sessions, editing them and uploading them to his different networks. In other weeks he is hired to play up to twelve consecutive hours or to attend in-person events in Colombia or abroad. He has invested more than 10,000–12,000 USD in his equipment — a Sony ZV-10 camera and a gaming PC — around 50 million Colombian pesos, which he considers a small part of the results his creativity has produced.

Marinilla and streaming setup

More than two years ago, Brayan moved from Cartagena to Marinilla, in the eastern region of Antioquia, where he designed and equipped a gaming setup in his home in the María Auxiliadora neighborhood, in the heart of town. He chose the Oriente antioqueño for its tranquility, its climate and the support of his partner's family — a young woman from Marinilla who grew up in Cartagena, where they met.

"You need very good equipment, a very good connection and an environment that gives you peace of mind to focus on getting the best performance," he told Vivir en El Poblado in 2026. He describes himself as a person who enjoys traveling, practicing extreme adventures and creating content while enjoying life, and he defines his content as a source of empathy and motivation for many young people.

Recognition and digital presence

Brayan Crazzy is recognized as an international reference of Free Fire content, with a community of more than 4 million followers across TikTok, Facebook, YouTube and Instagram — networks where his humor and his gamer persona have established a consolidated personal brand. His path has also allowed him to diversify his income into digital business ventures, building a patrimony estimated at more than 1,000 million Colombian pesos by his early years.

His content has been covered by national and international media, positioning him as "one of the best-connected inhabitants" of the gamer industry in Colombia, in the words of the regional press. He defines himself not as a materialistic person but as someone who does things with love and conviction, believing that his content can help many young people find a focus in life.
