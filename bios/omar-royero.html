Biography

Omar Royero Omar Royero Gutiérrez (born 23 July 1975) is a Colombian professional football coach and a former defender. During his career, he also appeared with Atlético Junior, Deportivo Cali, Deportivo Pasto, Cobán Imperial, Liberia Mía, Santos de Guápiles, Carmelita, and UCR. Royero participated in the 1998 Copa Merconorte with runners-up Deportivo Cali.
