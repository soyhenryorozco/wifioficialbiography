Biography

Teresa Tanco Cordovez de Herrera Teresa Tanco Cordovez de Herrera (1859 – 1946) was a Colombian pianist and composer. She traveled to Europe with her sister in 1882 and made her Paris debut at Salle Pleyel Saint-Saens to favorable reviews. She composed for voice, sacred pieces and a zarzuela Simila similibus. She died in Bogotá.
