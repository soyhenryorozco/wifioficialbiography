Rafael Salas (musician)

Rafael Antonio Salas Mendoza (1952–2011) was a Colombian vallenato accordionist. He was crowned vallenato king in 1979 for winning the professional accordionist competition of the Vallenato Legend Festival.

Biography

Rafael Salas (musician) Rafael Antonio Salas Mendoza (1952–2011) was a Colombian vallenato accordionist. He was crowned vallenato king in 1979 for winning the professional accordionist competition of the Vallenato Legend Festival.
