Natalia París

Natalia París Gaviria (born August 12, 1973) is a Colombian model, businesswoman, DJ and publicist. She has been one of the most recognized models in Colombia, owner of a brand of personal care products which bears her name.

Biography

Natalia París Natalia París Gaviria (born August 12, 1973) is a Colombian model, businesswoman, DJ and publicist. She has been one of the most recognized models in Colombia, owner of a brand of personal care products which bears her name.
