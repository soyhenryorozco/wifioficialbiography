Jacinto Navarrete

Jacinto Germán Navarrete Rodríguez (born 1 August 1962) is a Colombian retired athlete who competed in middle-distance and cross-country events. He represented his country at two World Indoor Championships and currently holds the South American record in the indoor 3000 metres.

Biography

Jacinto Navarrete Jacinto Germán Navarrete Rodríguez (born 1 August 1962) is a Colombian retired athlete who competed in middle-distance and cross-country events. He represented his country at two World Indoor Championships and currently holds the South American record in the indoor 3000 metres.
