Biography

Adrià Soriano Barrera Adrià Soriano Barrera (born 2 June 1999) is a Spanish-born Colombian professional tennis player. He has a career-high ATP singles ranking of No. 289 achieved on 28 July 2025 and a best doubles ranking of No. 262 achieved on 30 March 2026. He is currently the No. 3 Colombian player. Soriano Barrera played college tennis at University of Miami. He currently represents Colombia at the Davis Cup, where he has a win/loss record of 2–3.
