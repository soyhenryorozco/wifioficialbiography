Biography

Charlie Mirabal Charlie Peter Mirabal (born April 2, 1987) is a Venezuelan born Colombian former professional baseball infielder. Listed at 5 feet 11 inches (1.80 m) and 180 pounds (82 kg), Mirabal throws right-handed and is a switch hitter. He has played for the Colombia national baseball team and the Los Angeles Dodgers organization in Minor League Baseball.
