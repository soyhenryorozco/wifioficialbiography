Biography

Nixon Perea Nixon Perea Mosquera (born 15 August 1973) is a Colombian football coach and former player who played as a midfielder. He is the current manager of Peruvian club Alianza Lima's youth sides. Perea has played for, Independiente Santa Fe (Colombia), Atlético Nacional (Colombia), and Vegalta Sendai (Japan). He played for the Colombia national team at 1993 FIFA World Youth Championship in Australia.
