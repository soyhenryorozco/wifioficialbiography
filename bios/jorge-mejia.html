Biography

Jorge Mejia Jorge Andres Mejia (born October 27, 1972) is a Colombian-born American composer, pianist, singer-songwriter and music industry executive. He was nominated for a Latin Grammy Award in 2018 for "Prelude in F Major for Piano and Orchestra" from An Open Book: A Memoir in Music. Jorge is currently President & CEO at Sony Music Publishing for Latin America and U.S. Latin. He is an alum of the New World School of the Arts, the New England Conservatory of Music, and the University of Miami, from which he is a Distinguished Alumni.
