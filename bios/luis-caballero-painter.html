Biography

Luis Caballero (painter) Luis Caballero Holguín (27 August 1943 – 19 June 1995) was a Colombian painter, watercolourist, pastellist and lithographer. Caballero is known for depicting masculine figures, which often include both erotic and violent imagery. He is viewed as one of the most important figures in Colombian art.
