Biography

Camilo Becerra Camilo Becerra (born 10 October 1980) is a three-time Olympic freestyle swimmer from Colombia. He swam from Colombia at the 2000, 2004 and 2008 Olympics. The 2004 Games saw his highest Olympic finish to date: a tie for 37th in the men's 50m freestyle. Becerra also swam at the 2007 World Championships. He graduated from Southern Methodist University in 2006 with a degree in Finance and Economics. Becerra swims for the Fort Worth Area Swim team in Fort Worth Texas and is coached by Ron Forrest.
