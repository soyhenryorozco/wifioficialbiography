Julián Molina

Julián Molina (born 24 November 1998) is a Colombian professional BMX rider who has gained recognition at the X Games. He has represented Colombia in international competitions. He competed in the time trial event and the racing event at the Sosh Urban Motion in Paris 2015.

Biography

Julián Molina Julián Molina (born 24 November 1998) is a Colombian professional BMX rider who has gained recognition at the X Games. He has represented Colombia in international competitions. He competed in the time trial event and the racing event at the Sosh Urban Motion in Paris 2015.
