Biography

Diana Uribe Diana Uribe Forero (born March 30, 1959) is a Colombian radio host, podcaster, and writer specializing in world history. From 2000 to 2018, she hosted the Sunday educational program La historia del mundo (lit. 'The History of the World'), first on RadioNet then on Caracol Radio. Since 2018, she has produced the podcast on history and current events DianaUribe.fm. She has also written and narrated several books and audiobooks on world history.
