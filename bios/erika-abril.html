Erika Abril

Erika Abril Suárez (born March 29, 1978) is a Colombian long-distance runner. She competed in the marathon at the 2012 Summer Olympics, placing 51st with a time of 2:33:33, a Colombian national record.

Biography

Erika Abril Erika Abril Suárez (born March 29, 1978) is a Colombian long-distance runner. She competed in the marathon at the 2012 Summer Olympics, placing 51st with a time of 2:33:33, a Colombian national record.
