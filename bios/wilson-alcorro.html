Wilson Alcorro

Wilson Alcorro (born 11 March 1974) is a Colombian former professional boxer who competed from 1994 to 2011. He held the IBF Latino title in 1996.

Biography

Wilson Alcorro Wilson Alcorro (born 11 March 1974) is a Colombian former professional boxer who competed from 1994 to 2011. He held the IBF Latino title in 1996.
