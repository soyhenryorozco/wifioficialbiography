Otto Gutierrez

Otto Gutiérrez (born October 19, 1963) is a Colombian journalist who served as the press secretary of the President of Colombia from 1998 to 2000 and January to October 2013. He also served as the Consul General of Colombia in Houston, Texas from 2000 to 2002.

Biography

Otto Gutierrez Otto Gutiérrez (born October 19, 1963) is a Colombian journalist who served as the press secretary of the President of Colombia from 1998 to 2000 and January to October 2013. He also served as the Consul General of Colombia in Houston, Texas from 2000 to 2002.
