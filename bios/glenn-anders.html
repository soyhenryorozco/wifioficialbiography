Biography

Glenn Anders Glenn Anders (September 1, 1889 – October 26, 1981) was an American actor, most notable for his work on the stage. He was trained as an actor at the Wallace Dramatic School in his native city of Los Angeles. There he began his career in repertory theatre before touring in the Orpheum Circuit in vaudeville.
