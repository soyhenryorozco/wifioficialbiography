Manuela Paví

Manuela Paví Sepúlveda (Spanish: [maˈnwela paˈβi seˈpulβeða]; born 23 December 2000) is a Colombian professional footballer who plays as a forward for Liga MX Femenil club Deportivo Toluca F.C. (women) and the Colombia women's national team.
