Biography

Yaiselle Tous Yaiselle Tous Tejada (born 22 August 1996) is a Colombian model, psychologist and beauty pageant titleholder who participated in Señorita Colombia 2018, where she won the title of Tercera Princesa (4thrunner up). She represented Colombia at Miss Supranational 2019 and placed in the Top 10.
