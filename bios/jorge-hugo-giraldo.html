Jorge Hugo Giraldo

Jorge Hugo Giraldo López (born 5 September 1979) is a Colombian artistic gymnast and part of the national team. He participated at the 2004 Summer Olympics, 2008 Summer Olympics and 2012 Summer Olympics.
