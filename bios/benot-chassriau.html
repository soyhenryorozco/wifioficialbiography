Biography

Benoît Chassériau Benoît Chassériau (also known as Benito Chassériau or Chasserieux; 19 August 1780 – 27 September 1844) was a French diplomat and spy. He was the Minister of the Interior of Cartagena, Colombia and comrade in arms of Simón Bolívar. He was the father of the artist Théodore Chassériau.
