Biography

Paula Ávila-Guillén Paula Avila-Guillen is an American-Colombian lawyer, specialist in human rights, and sexual and reproductive rights activist in Latin America. Currently, she is the Executive Director of the Women's Equality Center. At La-WEC, she collaborates with Latin-American organizations and leaders working for reproductive freedom in the region, designing strategies such as legal defense tactics and communications campaigns.
