Biography

Arnovis Dalmero Arnovis de Jesús Dalmero Arvilla (born 23 September 2000) is a Colombian athlete specialising in the long jump. He won a gold medal at the 2023 Pan American Games. His personal bests in the event are 8.20 metres outdoors (+0.8 m/s, Bogotá 2023) and 8.16 metres indoors (Cochabamba 2024). Both are current national records.
