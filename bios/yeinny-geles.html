Biography

Yeinny Geles Yeinny Norela Geles Moreno (born 6 December 1997) is a Colombian weightlifter. She won the silver medal in the women's 87 kg event at the 2023 World Weightlifting Championships held in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. She is a two-time medalist at the Pan American Weightlifting Championships. She won two medals at the 2022 Bolivarian Games held in Valledupar, Colombia. Geles competed in the girls' +63 kg event at the 2014 Summer Youth Olympics held in Nanjing, China. She won the bronze medal in her event at the 2022 South American Games held in Asunción, Paraguay.
