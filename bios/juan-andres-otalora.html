Biography

Juan Andres Otalora Juan Andres Otalora is a Colombian film and TV composer, arranger and guitarist. He won a Latin Grammy Award in 2014 for producing and arranging the album Fonseca Sinfónico. Born in Bogota, he majored in jazz guitar from Pontifical Xavierian University, while still enrolled he began touring with artists such as Maia, Compañia Ilimitada and eventually Fonseca for 8 years. In 2014, Otalora arranged and produced Sinfonico with Fonseca and the National Symphony Orchestra of Colombia (OSNC) which won a Latin Grammy Award for "Best Contemporary Pop Vocal Album" and was nominated for "Album of the Year".
