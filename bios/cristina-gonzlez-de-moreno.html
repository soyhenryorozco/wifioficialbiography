Biography

Cristina González de Moreno Cristina González Villegas de Moreno (born c. 1962) is a Colombian model and lawyer who served as First Lady of Bogotá from 2008 to 2012 during the mayoralty of Samuel Moreno. Born in Pereira, Risaralda, González de Moreno studied law at the University of La Sabana, where she met Samuel in 1979 when they were taking a class together.
