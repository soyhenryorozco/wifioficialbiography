Biography

Yu Takeuchi (竹内 悠, Takeuchi Yū) (16 March 1927, Tokyo – 25 December 2014, Bogotá) was a Colombian nationalized Japanese physicist and mathematician, teacher and promoter of mathematics in Colombia.
