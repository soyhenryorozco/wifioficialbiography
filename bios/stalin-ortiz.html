Biography

Stalin Ortiz Stalin Ortiz (born February 8, 1981), is a Colombian professional basketball player. He currently plays for the Condores club of the Baloncesto Profesional Colombiano league. He represented Colombia's national basketball team at the 2016 South American Basketball Championship, where he had most minutes, points and steals for his team.
