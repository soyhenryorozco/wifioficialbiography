Biography

Víctor Manuel Muñoz Víctor Manuel Muñoz Rodríguez is a Colombian industrial engineer and public official who served as Director of the Administrative Department of the Presidency of Colombia under President Iván Duque from February 2021 to August 2022. He previously served as High Presidential Advisor for Innovation and Digital Transformation. Muñoz holds a degree in Industrial Engineering from Pontificia Universidad Javeriana and an MBA from Universidad de los Andes.
