Francisco Mejia-Guinand

Francisco Mejía-Guinand (born 1964 Bogotá, Colombia) is a New York-based architect and visual artist. Mejía-Guinand gained prominence in the 1990s as an artist who merged architecture with sculpture and painting.

Biography

Francisco Mejia-Guinand Francisco Mejía-Guinand (born 1964 Bogotá, Colombia) is a New York-based architect and visual artist. Mejía-Guinand gained prominence in the 1990s as an artist who merged architecture with sculpture and painting.
