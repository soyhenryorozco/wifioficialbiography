Jorge Iván González

Jorge Iván González Borrero (born March 29, 1953) is a Colombian philosopher, economist and university professor. From August 2022 to February 2024 he served as director of the National Planning Department, the third most important cabinet-level position in the National Government.

Biography

Jorge Iván González Jorge Iván González Borrero (born March 29, 1953) is a Colombian philosopher, economist and university professor. From August 2022 to February 2024 he served as director of the National Planning Department, the third most important cabinet-level position in the National Government.
