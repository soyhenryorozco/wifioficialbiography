Biography

Marco Tulio Aguilera Garramuño Marco Tulio Aguilera Garramuño (born 27 February 1949) is a Colombian novelist, critic and journalist. Born in Bogotá, he studied philosophy at the University of Valle and literature at the University of Kansas. Since then, he has lived in Costa Rica and Mexico, where he has taught at the Universidad Veracruzana. He has published around 40 books, and has won numerous awards. His first novel, Breve historia de todas las cosas, written when he was 24 year old, was compared with Cien años de soledad. His book Cuentos para hacer el amor (1983) has been recognized as one of the best Colombian works of fiction of the 20th century.
