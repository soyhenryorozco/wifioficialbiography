Miguel Ángel Sanabria

Miguel Ángel Sanabria Acevedo (September 20, 1967 – October 7, 2006) was a male road cyclist from Colombia, who was a professional from 1990 to 1998. He was born in Tuta, Boyacá, and nicknamed "El Ratón" during his career.

Biography

Miguel Ángel Sanabria Miguel Ángel Sanabria Acevedo (September 20, 1967 – October 7, 2006) was a male road cyclist from Colombia, who was a professional from 1990 to 1998. He was born in Tuta, Boyacá, and nicknamed "El Ratón" during his career.
